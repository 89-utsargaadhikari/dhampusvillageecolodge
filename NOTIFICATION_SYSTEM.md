## 🖱️ HOW TO USE

### **View Notifications:**
1. Click the bell icon 🔔
2. Dropdown panel shows all notifications
3. Newest notifications at the top

### **Read Notifications:**
- Click any notification to mark as read
- If it has a link, you'll be taken to that page
- Click "Mark all read" to clear all

### **Delete Notifications:**
- Click the trash icon 🗑️ on any notification
- Or let them auto-cleanup (keeps last 50)
